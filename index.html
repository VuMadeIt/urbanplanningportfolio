YZD
Downsview

100 questions a day from neighbours

Northcrest Developments · YZD Senior Ambassador, Engagement · February 2025 to now

The place

The former Downsview airport lands in north Toronto, where Northcrest is planning new neighbourhoods. It is also where Liam grew up: an essay on what the airport site should become, written for a scholarship in grade 12, is what led him to planning.

The work

  • Explains the YZD redevelopment to residents and visitors at the YZD Experience Centre, answering about 100 questions a day.
  • Organized a guided tour of the Northcrest office and the Experience Centre for TMU planning students.
  • Trained on the Downsview Secondary Plan and the Framework Plan for the Downsview lands.

The
Meadoway

16 km of hydro corridor becoming meadow

Toronto and Region Conservation Authority · Watershed Planning and Ecosystem Science, summer student and media intern · Summer 2026

The place

A 16-kilometre hydro corridor in Scarborough being turned into meadow, trail and habitat, linking downtown Toronto with Rouge National Urban Park.

The work

  • Spent four months documenting TRCA’s field teams in photos and short videos for TRCA’s channels.
  • Joined bird monitoring in the Meadoway at 4:30 a.m., spring ephemeral surveys at Guild Park and the Spring Bird Festival at Tommy Thompson Park.
  • TRCA published his summer as the photo story Through the Lens: Liam Vu.

Art by Liam Vu

Liam paints outdoors, sketches Toronto’s third spaces and teaches urban sketching. He founded the Oh Canada Art Show.

Next: About

Painted live at ACTION 2025

At Canada’s national planning conference in Toronto, Liam pitched himself to the organizers and painted the skyline live in front of over 1,000 planners. It was his first live painting. That evening at Roy Thomson Hall, guests wrote messages on the finished canvas.

“With my paints, brushes, and a huge wooden French easel, I completed a painting of the Toronto skyline.”

Liam, to the Canadian Institute of Planners

On the wall

More of his work on @liamvuart

Sketchbook first

He sketches the places planners talk about: libraries, diners, plazas and greenhouses where people spend time outside home and work. The sketches become reels, and the reels start conversations about third spaces.

How he works

Plein air
Painting outside, on location, with a French easel, from parks to the skyline.
Urban sketching
Drawing Toronto’s third spaces, from the Reference Library to Sneaky Dee’s, and teaching it: he led an urban sketching workshop for plazaPOPS.
Workshops
Free sketching workshops in public space, like Sketching Street Life at Yonge-Dundas in Sankofa Square, May 2026.
Shows
Founder of the Oh Canada Art Show. Its free 2025 showcase, Allyship Through Music and Dance, was hosted with the Toronto Catholic District School Board.

About Liam

  • Based in Toronto, Canada
  • Studying since 2024
  • Reach him liamvuplanning@gmail.com

Liam Vu is an award-winning planning student at TMU and a Council Director at the Ontario Professional Planners Institute.

He found planning in grade 12, when a developer came to his high school in Downsview to ask students what the old airport lands should become. Today he explains city-building to residents in person and to thousands of people online.

Outside work and school you will find him volunteering with advocacy groups, at networking events, painting outdoors, learning K-pop choreography, or playing with his two-year-old brother, Duncan.
